# Buffett capital-allocation lens (summary)

1. **Retain earnings** only if the business can reinvest at high ROIC; otherwise distribute (dividends/buybacks).
2. **Prefer economic moats** that sustain ROE/ROIC ≥15% without needing heavy leverage.
3. **Owner earnings** (cash) over accounting cosmetics; distrust ROE that is mostly equity multiplier.
4. **Fair price for wonderful companies** — multiple expansion is acceptable when durability is high (explains dual-screen rarity).
5. **Circle of competence** — avoid complexity you cannot underwrite (e.g. speculative launch cadence).
6. **Position sizing** — larger where predictability is highest; our stack maps this to quality_score + vol caps (SMCI ≤5%).

DuPont (`dupont_analysis.py`) separates operating ROE from leverage-driven ROE to operationalize (2)–(3).
